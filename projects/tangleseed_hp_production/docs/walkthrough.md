# TangleSeed Official HP: Project Walkthrough

This project focused on designing the **TangleSeed Official HP** to rank #1 for "ゼンタングル" in Japan while aligning with TangleSeed's brand mission and business goals.

## 1. Accomplishments

### 🌍 Global & Local Research
- Identified major global players (Zentangle.com, TanglePatterns.com) and 2025 trends (Pattern of the Year: Huggins, "Neurological Hack" messaging).
- Analyzed the Japanese competitive landscape, finding a gap in authoritative, centralized pattern libraries (currently fragmented across personal blogs).

### 🤝 Internal Knowledge Integration
- Integrated **TangleSeed's** core mission: "Art as a healing force for those in difficult situations."
- Incorporated powerful social proof (40,000 users, 8-year cancer patient support history).
- Aligned site architecture with the **B2B Alliance Strategy** (Bookstore packages, Postcard QR method).

### 🎨 Strategic Site Design
- **Premium Zen Aesthetic**: Defined a high-end visual style combining traditional Japanese sensitivity with modern mindfulness.
- **Conversion-Centric Architecture**: Designed the "7-Day LINE Challenge" as the primary user funnel.
- **B2B Credibility**: Created a dedicated Alliance Hub to build trust with corporate partners like Shiseido or major bookstores.
